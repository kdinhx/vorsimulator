PILOT'S VOR / DME CHEAT SHEET & RULES

What is a Radial?

Radials always project OUTWARD (FROM) the station like spokes on a bicycle wheel from 000° to 359° magnetic. If you are directly East of the station, you are on the 090° Radial, regardless of which way the airplane's nose is pointed!

TO / FROM Flag Logic

The indicator compares your selected OBS course to your position:

  • FROM: Plane is in the half-hemisphere towards which the selected OBS arrow points.
  • TO: Plane is in the reciprocal hemisphere (flying this course takes you TO the VOR).
  • NAV / OFF: 90° abeam the course line or in the cone of confusion overhead.

Avoiding "Reverse Sensing"

Reverse sensing happens when your aircraft heading is roughly opposite to the dialed OBS course. The needle points right, but you must steer left to intercept!

Golden Rule: When flying INBOUND to a VOR, always set the OBS to the inbound course (bearing TO the station). When flying OUTBOUND, set the OBS to the radial (FROM)!

DME Slant-Range Geometry

DME transmits radar pulses that measure hypotenuse line-of-sight distance. Directly over the station at 6,076 ft (1 NM altitude), your DME will read exactly 1.0 NM, not 0.0!
